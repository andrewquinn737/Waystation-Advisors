# How the AI coach works (data sales)

## The three steps on this tab

1. **Connect Claude or ChatGPT.** Copy your AI link into Claude (Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector) or ChatGPT.
   Then you can say "coach me" or "run a practice call" there too. The link is personal: don't share it.
2. **Your coach.** Every real call where you reach someone gets graded a few minutes after you hang up: an opener score,
   an objection score, what to keep and what to fix, and one line to try next time. After every 10 graded
   conversations the coach rewrites your advice at the top, based on those 10 calls.
3. **Practice calls.** You call an AI owner built from one of our real calls. It answers the way that owner did.

## How practice gets harder

- **Opening lines.** Until your first three lines land, the practice owner shuts you down fast, just like most real
  owners do. Pass three openers in a row to move on.
- **Objections.** The practice owner pushes back with the objections you handle worst on your real calls, until you
  handle them well.
- **Full calls.** Whole calls from hello to the ask.

It only gets easier when you get better. A practice owner that hangs up on you is telling you the opener didn't land.

## The opener that works best on our calls

"Hey [first name]. My name is [your name]. I'm calling because I have a bit of a weird request. I'm researching
[industry], trying to understand your process. I thought [company] would be a good one to reach out to because you
guys have been around [X] years. I'd like to pay to access any project records you have, like [example 1] or
[example 2]. Do you know how much it would cost to do something like that?"

- Your name first, then the reason. Owners answer a person, not a brand they've never heard of.
- "Weird request" owns the strangeness before they can.
- "I'd like to pay" keeps the frame right: you are offering money, not selling.
- Don't ask "do you have a minute?". It kept nobody on the line in our calls.
- Save Anthropic and OpenAI for when they ask what the records are for.

## When they push back

- **"What is this?"** One plain sentence: we pay businesses for a copy of old records, strip anything identifying, and
  license them to AI companies training their models.
- **Confidential / NDA.** Customer names and anything identifying come out before anything leaves their hands. Offer
  a short call with Arieh to walk through what goes in and what stays out.
- **Busy.** Get a specific day, time and the right person. Read it back.
- **Not interested, the second time.** Stop. "Totally fair. Thanks for picking up." Pushing after a second no almost
  never works and burns the lead.

## The ask

One ask only: a 15 minute call with Arieh. Check they want to keep talking first ("Does it make sense to talk a little
more about this?"), then offer two days. Never leave with "I'll try you again sometime."

## Getting the most out of it

- Talk out loud with the mic. Typing a perfect line isn't the same as saying it.
- Read the "line that cost you most" after each practice call and say the fix out loud before the next one.
- Five practice calls before your first dial of the day is a good warm-up.
