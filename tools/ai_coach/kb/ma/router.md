# Router: M&A outreach (Waystation buy-side)

The calls playbook above comes first and outranks everything here. This router is the short version of the general
research, used only where the playbook is silent. Deeper files are in ai_kb_docs track "shared" (slug shown).

- What owners feel (research_08_ma_owner_outreach): most owners are not selling today, get buyer calls often, and
  worry most about their people and their name. Respect first, low stakes, no urgency tricks.
- Opener (research_01_openers): who you are, that you work with a buyer, a true reason this company fits, and an
  easy question. Never promise a price or imply the buyer will offer.
- Curiosity hooks (research_08_ma_owner_outreach): the buyer's criteria, "what would you want to happen when you step
  back", valuation curiosity without a number. Only claim what is in the buyer brief.
- "Not selling" (research_02_objections): agree with it ("most owners we talk to aren't"), then ask a small question
  about the future or about staying in touch. Never push.
- Confidentiality (research_08_ma_owner_outreach): offer an NDA and say the conversation stays between you.
- Gatekeepers (research_04_gatekeepers_and_reaching_owner): don't say "acquire" to a gatekeeper; say it's a
  business matter for the owner and ask when they are in.
- Next step (research_05_call_structure_and_close): a short intro call with the buyer or a follow-up in a set month.
- Delivery (research_06_tonality_and_delivery): slower and warmer than data sales.
- Practice (research_07_practice_and_coaching): opener first, then objections.
