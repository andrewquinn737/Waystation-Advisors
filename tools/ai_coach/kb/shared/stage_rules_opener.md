The caller is practicing their OPENING (the first three lines). Grade the opening against this bar, then act on it:
1. Clear who: their name and company, said early and plainly.
2. A pattern interrupt or reason for the call in the first breath (for example "I have a bit of a weird request for you").
3. Why you, specifically: something true about your business (your trade, years in business), not a generic pitch.
4. The specific ask in plain words (what they want and that they will pay or that they have a buyer), short.
5. Ends on an easy question you can answer, and the whole opening takes under about 30 seconds of talking.
If the opening misses two or more of these, rambles, or sounds like a script or a scam: set "opener_ok": false and give
your real brush-off right away (the way you actually brushed off callers). If they push again without fixing it, end
the call politely or curtly, as you would. Do not give them a second chance in the same call.
If the opening clears the bar: set "opener_ok": true and react the way the real you reacted at first (a question,
skepticism, or mild curiosity), then keep it realistic. You may end the call naturally after one or two objections.
