Add mailboxes

Paste one mailbox per line (CSV or tab-separated, e.g. from a spreadsheet): email, password, display name, owner, limit, mandate. Only email and password are required. Owner can be an email or a full name; with none, the display name is matched to a person, else it goes to Unassigned. Hosts are filled in for Microsoft 365 and Google automatically.
